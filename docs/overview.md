# Notes

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Notes lets your team leave private notes on records in the admin, with optional reminders. Notes are for your team only and are never shown to visitors.

## Your screens

- **Notes**: the inbox of notes across your records.
- **A note on a record**: the note attached to a specific item.

## What you can do

- Add a private note to a record.
- See recent notes in one place.
- Get a reminder when a note is due.

## Where to find it

Open **Notes** in the admin, or add a note directly on a record.

## Good to know

- Leave a private **note** on a record for your team.
- Notes are internal and never shown to visitors.
- A note can carry a reminder, so nothing important is forgotten.
