## What it does for you

Notes lets your team leave internal notes on supported admin records. Use them to hand work to an editor, @mention someone who needs context, and set a reminder for follow-up. Notes are never shown to visitors.

## Your screens

- **Notes**: your inbox of assigned notes, due and overdue reminders, and @mentions across records you can edit.
- **A note on a record**: the note, its assignees, mentions, reminder, and completion state on a specific item.

## What you can do

- Add a private or record-editor note to a record.
- Assign one or more editors and @mention people who need to see the note.
- Mark your assignment complete, or resolve and later reopen a note.
- Set a one-off, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly reminder.
- See the notes that need your attention in one place.

## Where to find it

Open **Notes** in the admin, or add a note directly on a record.

## Good to know

- Notes are internal and never shown to visitors. Everyone who can see a note must also be allowed to edit its record.
- Choose **Record editors** when other editors of the record should see the note. Choose **Private** when only the author, active assignees, and mentioned people should see it.
- Assignments and @mentions notify the relevant editors. An assignee can complete only their own assignment; completing it does not complete anyone else's.
- Resolving a note records its resolution time. The author or an active assignee can reopen it if work resumes.
- Reminders notify active assignees when due. With the default configuration, the scheduler checks for them every five minutes; keep the application scheduler running for reminders to be sent.
